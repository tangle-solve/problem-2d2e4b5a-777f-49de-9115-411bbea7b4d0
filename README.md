### BCGT-000312: Free-option-resistant PBS under strategic failures
- **Subfield:** PBS market structure, inclusion and builder infrastructure
- **Open:** Limit payload withholding options when builders and proposers hold outside positions; price collateral, payment and fallback rules accordingly.
- **Status:** Research target extending EIP-7732, which remained in Review on the snapshot date.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Trustless payment does not price every abandonment incentive.
